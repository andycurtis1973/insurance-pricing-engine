Renewal / switch · a guided walkthrough

Win the renewal: bring your current policy, the engine finds the deal that keeps you

Each customer arrives with a policy they already have. They're one click from a cheaper competitor. The engine works its one real lever — coverage traded against price — two ways: trim for a lower rate to keep a price shopper, or add coverage for a higher rate one who'll pay. It shows both and plays whichever wins the deal. Pick a customer and press play.

Current policy is an input (a live system has it at renewal). The coverage↔price trade-off is calibrated to the real Allstate relationship (~$14/level); acceptance is modeled.
